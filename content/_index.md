The Dubal Lab is an **aging and neuroscience laboratory** at UCSF. We seek to understand how to slow aging, strengthen brain resilience, and prevent diseases of the aging brain, such as Alzheimer’s disease. Our research advances fundamental discoveries in longevity science and in women’s health, with the goal of **improving health and quality of life** as we age.
