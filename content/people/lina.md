Lina received her B.A. in Molecular and Cellular Biology from the University of California, where she studied glucocorticoid function in beige adipocytes. She is currently a student in the Developmental and Stem Cell Biology program at UCSF. Currently, her research focuses on investigating liver-derived factors, their interactions with the hippocampus, and how these interactions influence brain aging and cognitive function.

Outside of lab, Lina enjoys cycling, going to concerts, and trying new restaurants in San Francisco.
