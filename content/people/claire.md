Claire earned her M.Sc. in Pharmacology and Physiology from King’s College London and her Ph.D. from the Open University UK, conducting research at the Roskamp Institute on lipid metabolism in Alzheimer’s disease (AD). Her research identified early changes in lipid metabolism associated with AD pathology and APOE genotype. As a postdoctoral fellow in the Dubal lab, she investigates the aging kidney-brain axis and the longevity factor klotho to identify circulating factors and mechanisms of inter-organ communication that promote cognitive resilience. Outside the lab, Claire enjoys reading, nature walks, and exploring delicious foods with friends and family.
