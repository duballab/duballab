Dan Wang received her MD in Pediatrics from China Medical University and her MS in Clinical Laboratory Science from San Francisco State University. Before joining the Dubal Lab, she worked at the Ernest Gallo Clinic and Research Center, where she studied alcoholism. Her research interests include investigating sex differences in neurodegenerative diseases and understanding the role of Klotho in health and disease. Outside the lab, she enjoys camping, hiking, and sewing.
