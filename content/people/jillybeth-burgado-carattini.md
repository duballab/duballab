Jillybeth Burgado Carattini is a postdoctoral fellow in the Dubal lab investigating how the X chromosome influences brain aging, Alzheimer’s disease, and female brain resilience. She earned her Ph.D. in Dr. Nicola Allen’s lab at the Salk Institute, where she studied astrocyte contributions to Alzheimer’s disease. Her earlier research at the CDC and Emory University spanned infectious disease surveillance, metabolism, stress, and sex differences in brain health. Passionate about mentorship and expanding opportunities for underserved communities, Jillybeth also enjoys camping, hiking, traveling, and spending time with family.
