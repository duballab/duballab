Beril Erdogdu is a BCBI Postdoctoral Fellow between UC Berkeley and UCSF whose research focuses on developing computational and statistical approaches to understand how transcriptomic regulation shapes human brain development, aging, and disease. At the Dubal lab, she aims to build predictive models of neurodegenerative disease based on cell-free DNA.
