Francesca received her B.A. in Neuroscience from Boston University where she used electrophysiology to study learning and memory in Dr. Howard Eichenbaum’s lab. She then moved to Edmund Hollis’ lab at Burke Neurological Institute to evaluate the role of corticospinal neurons in motor learning and spinal cord injury. In the Dubal lab, she is studying the role of the X-chromosome in cognitive aging and neurodegenerative disease.

Outside of the lab, Francesca enjoys hiking, playing board games, video games, and reading.
