Chen earned her bachelor’s degree in Preventive Medicine from the School of Medicine at Wuhan University of Science and Technology and later obtained her master’s degree in public health from the Beijing Institute of Medicine. Before joining the Dubal Lab, she worked at the Center for Disease Control in Tai’an City, China.

Her research interests include aging, sexual dimorphism, and neurodegeneration, with a particular focus on animal behavior and molecular biology. She is also eager to expand her knowledge and develop new research skills and techniques.

In her spare time, Chen enjoys cooking, shopping, and spending time playing with her lovely sons.
