Nilla Sivakumar is a graduate student in the Dubal Lab studying how the X chromosome is regulated in aging male and female brains. She holds a bachelor's degree in neuroscience with minors in history and biomedical research from UCLA, where she studied estrogen-sensitive neurons of the hypothalamus under the supervision of Dr. Stephanie Correa. She previously worked in the lab of Dr. Zachary Knight, studying the brainstem's control of appetite. Outside of lab, she can be found haunting local libraries and movie theaters.
