Veronika grew up in Portland, OR, but moved to the East Coast to earn her BS and MS degrees at George Washington University, where she studied Aβ and tau pathology in aged mammalian brains in Dr. Chet Sherwood’s lab. After graduating, she joined Dr. Jessica Tollkuhn’s lab at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, where she used a mouse model of menopause to study how hormone depletion affects gene programs in the brain. Now in the Dubal lab, she is co-mentored by Dr. Ryan Corces at the Gladstone Institutes. Her project focuses on understanding differences between the maternal and paternal X chromosomes in the aging brain.
