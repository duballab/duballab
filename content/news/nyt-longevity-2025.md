Women outlive men worldwide, even during famines and epidemics, but they do not necessarily spend those extra years in good health. Scientists are working to understand why the sexes age differently, in the hope of extending lifespan and healthspan for everyone. Dr. Dubal describes her lab's mouse research, in which animals with two X chromosomes lived longest, even when they had testes, which points to a protective effect of the second X chromosome.
