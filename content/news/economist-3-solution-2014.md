Dr. Dubal and Dr. Lennart Mucke studied KL-VS, a version of the klotho gene linked to longevity, in 220 older adults, and compared their results with two other studies. People carrying KL-VS scored better on cognitive tests, a gap the article estimates at about six IQ points. Mice engineered to have more klotho learned and remembered better, with changes in the brain's NMDA receptors. The authors suggest that a drug that raises klotho could one day enhance cognition.
