An early experiment in older rhesus macaques suggests that an injection of klotho improves working memory. Researchers at Yale and UCSF found that a single injection of the klotho protein led to modest improvements in cognitive function in older monkeys, and that the effects lasted for two weeks. Could it one day help people?
