A growing group of female scientists, entrepreneurs and influencers is entering a longevity field long led by men. Women live about five years longer than men in the U.S., an advantage seen in every country with mortality data, yet they spend more of their later years in poor health. Dr. Dubal is quoted on this long-standing puzzle. Researchers see the study of ovaries, hormones and menopause as a way to unlock the biology of aging for both sexes.
